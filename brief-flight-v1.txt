## The game (original; no commercial game's names/art/levels)
Golden-hour archipelago, one coherent low-poly style: warm sun, teal sea, green-and-tan islands, soft fog, gradient sky. Player flies the fictional "Kestrel K-1" single-prop bush plane.

World (game/src/world.js): seeded procedural archipelago on a 4000x4000 m sea — a heightmap island cluster (main island + 5 smaller), vertex-colored terrain (sand/grass/rock by height and slope), animated water plane, gradient sky dome + sun, drifting low-poly clouds, cone trees and rocks scattered by the seeded RNG, a lighthouse, and a 600 m runway on the main island (dark strip, white threshold markings, edge lights) on flat ground near sea level.

Aircraft (game/src/aircraft.js): Kestrel K-1 built from three.js primitives with flat shading — fuselage, high wing with struts, tail, spinning prop (blur disc + blades), fixed gear with wheels, glass canopy, cream fuselage with red livery stripes. It must look built, not like a single box.

Cockpit (game/src/cockpit.js): first-person cockpit — frame pillars, glareshield, yoke that tilts with input, and a panel with SIX live round gauges drawn on canvas textures and updated every frame: airspeed, altimeter, attitude indicator (moves with pitch/roll), heading, vertical speed, tachometer. Gauges have lit faces (bright dial, glowing needles) so screenshots read "instruments lit".

HUD (game/src/hud.js): DOM overlay, large readable type — ALT / SPD / HDG readouts top-left, fuel and hull bars top-right, mission banner top-center, controls hint bottom. Must stay readable when the page is shrunk to 768x480.

Flight model (game/src/flight.js): arcade but disciplined — throttle 0-100% (Shift/Ctrl or W/S), pitch (Up/Down), roll (Left/Right) inducing banked turns, rudder (A/D), lift rising with speed, stall below ~26 m/s (nose drop + shake), takeoff roll on the runway, terrain/water collision = crash, landing gear always down; gentle runway touchdown = success, hard touchdown = hull damage.

Missions (game/src/missions.js): a sortie loop — briefing card (mission name, goal, controls) -> takeoff -> fly through 10 glowing rings in order (next ring pulses, pass = chime + banner + counter) -> return and land on the runway before fuel runs out. Fuel drains with throttle; landing refuels and repairs. Hull damage from hard contact; hull 0 = crashed screen with restart. Score = rings + time bonus. Free-flight toggle (key F) hides the course.

Input (game/src/input.js): keyboard state + mouse (mouse X steers roll when held? keep simple: keys primary, mouse drag looks around in cockpit). Camera keys: C toggles cockpit / chase / tower-flyby.

Audio (game/src/audio.js): tiny WebAudio — engine hum tied to RPM, wind tied to speed, ring chime, touchdown thud; M mutes; start on first interaction. Keep it under ~80 lines.

Shot modes (game/src/shots.js): when the URL has ?shot=<name>&seed=7, set a deterministic staged scene and keep the world alive (prop spinning, water moving, gauges live) with physics frozen at the staged state:
- cover: COCKPIT view, 120 m over the sea heading at ring 3, main island and runway visible ahead-left, gauges lit, HUD showing real values. This is the cover: it must show cockpit + instruments + terrain + a ring ahead + runway in one frame.
- chase: CHASE view, aircraft banking ~25 degrees over a coastline, two rings ahead, golden light.
- approach: low chase view on short final, aircraft aligned with the runway, gear down, threshold lights ahead.
- briefing: the start/briefing screen over a slow scenic orbit of the archipelago (aircraft parked on the runway, course rings visible in the distance).
Normal load (no ?shot) starts at the briefing screen on the runway, engine off.

Main (game/src/main.js): boot, fixed-step loop, camera director, resize handling, pause on blur. Config (game/src/config.js): palette, sizes, tuning constants. Random (game/src/random.js): mulberry32 seeded RNG.
